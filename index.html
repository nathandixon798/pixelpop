FAQ

Questions we hear all the time.

Honest answers. If yours isn't here, just ask.

How long does a typical project take from start to launch?

Most websites go live within 2–4 weeks from kickoff. Bigger projects with custom booking, e-commerce, or lots of content can stretch to 6 weeks. We'll give you a clear timeline before we start, and you'll always know what's happening that week.

How much does a new website actually cost?

Projects start from £3,000 for a polished, custom-built website. Bigger builds with extras like online booking, e-commerce, or lots of content scale up from there. We send a fixed quote before any work starts — no hidden fees, no surprise invoices, no hourly creep.

I already have a website. Can you fix it instead of starting over?

Absolutely. We do free audits of existing sites and often the right call is to fix what's there rather than rebuild from scratch. We'll be straight with you — sometimes a rebuild really is the better investment, but we'll explain why before suggesting it.

Will my site actually rank on Google, or is that just sales talk?

For local searches in your area, yes — that's our specialty. SEO isn't magic, but for a local business competing with a handful of others in town, the right technical setup, optimised Google Business profile, and good content goes a long way. We'll be honest about realistic timelines (usually 2–3 months to start seeing real movement).

What happens after launch — am I on my own?

Never. Every site comes with 30 days of free tweaks and support post-launch. After that, we offer a simple monthly care plan covering ongoing SEO maintenance, security updates, content edits, and a monthly check-in on your traffic and rankings. You're free to walk away any time — your site and domain always stay yours.

Do you really donate 10% to charity, or is that marketing?

Real, every project, no asterisks. 10% of the revenue from every website we build goes directly to A21, an organisation fighting human trafficking and modern slavery. We'll happily share receipts on request — it's a small thing, but it's why we get out of bed.
